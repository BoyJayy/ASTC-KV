Dear Mara,

I have finally arranged the journey we discussed in the spring. I will reach the small coastal town on Thursday afternoon, provided that the bus from the inland station keeps to its usual route. The guesthouse owner has offered to leave a key with the bookshop next door. If the bookshop is closed, I am supposed to ring the bell beside the courtyard gate rather than the large bell at the front entrance. Apparently the front bell belongs to a different household.

I would like to spend Friday walking along the old harbour, but I have deliberately left the rest of the day unplanned. Last year I tried to visit every place in a guidebook and remembered mostly the time spent checking the next departure. This time I want to sit somewhere long enough to notice how the town changes through the day. You once described the fishing boats returning while the bakery was opening, and that ordinary overlap has stayed in my mind more vividly than any famous building.

Please do not prepare an elaborate dinner for my arrival. Bread, soup, and a quiet hour at your kitchen table would be more than enough. I am bringing the blue notebook you left at our apartment, along with the photographs from our walk in the hills. I have put the loose pages inside an envelope so that they do not fall out on the bus. There is also a drawing by my niece, who thinks every journey to the sea must involve finding a lighthouse.

On Saturday we could take the path above the western beach if you feel like walking. If the weather turns unpleasant, I would be equally happy to help you sort the boxes in your new studio. You mentioned that the labels came loose during the move. We could begin with the materials you need every week and leave the less familiar boxes until later. I know how easy it is to spend an afternoon organizing things that will not be used for months.

Send a short note when you have time, especially if the station road is still being repaired. Otherwise I will follow the directions you gave me and look for the bookshop with the green window frame. I am looking forward to seeing you without a timetable between us.

With affection,
Jonas
