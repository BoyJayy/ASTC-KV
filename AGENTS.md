# Report style

Write reports in clear Russian with ordinary keyboard punctuation.
Use the ASCII hyphen (-), straight quotes (" and '), ordinary spaces,
and ASCII notation for formulas and units: ->, <=, >=, *, us, delta.
Do not use long dashes, guillemets, curly quotes, nonbreaking spaces,
Unicode arrows, superscripts or decorative symbols in authored reports.
Russian letters are allowed. Keep code, numbers, sources and uncertainty accurate.

Keep original inputs and frozen raw results unchanged. Before revising a
previously frozen report, preserve its original bytes and record old/new hashes.
Do not claim that punctuation proves authorship or guarantees a detector result.
